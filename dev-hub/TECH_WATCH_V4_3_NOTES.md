# ChaCha DEV HUB — Technology Watch v4.3

## Why v4.3

The v4.2 transport and scheduler are healthy, but broad GitHub search can return repositories that merely match generic words and are not credible alternatives for the DEV HUB. Popularity is also not enough evidence to justify a pilot or migration.

## v4.3 policy

1. Installed and explicitly curated candidates remain the authoritative watch list.
2. Market discovery is only a discovery signal, never a recommendation.
3. Market results must pass category-specific relevance terms, minimum stars, recency, non-archived and non-fork checks.
4. Automatic discovery may emit `ASSESS` or `WATCH`; it may not emit `PILOT`, `RECOMMEND` or perform a migration.
5. `PILOT` and `RECOMMEND` require a later comparative evaluation against the installed baseline: functional fit, quality, security, maturity, performance, deployment compatibility, documentation, maintenance burden, cost, migration effort and rollback.
6. First-run `NEW_TARGET` events are bootstrap noise and are not treated as technology changes.
7. Reports expose source confidence so curated targets and open-market discoveries cannot be confused.

This keeps the radar useful as a CTO-style watch system rather than a popularity leaderboard.
